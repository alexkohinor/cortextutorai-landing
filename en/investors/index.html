For investors

A multimodal learning system that has cleared substantive examination

The system merges what is normally handled separately — video, speech with its intonation, and the course of the dialogue — into a single model of the learner, and changes how it teaches from that model rather than from a test at the end of a topic.

Stage: there is no product yet.

A first version and a pilot with 20–30 students are in preparation. Of the fourteen features in the patent claim, none is fully implemented: three are in development, three partial. We say this first so as not to waste your time.

Legal position

On invention application No. 2025118773, «Intelligent system of personalised learning with multimodal analysis and adaptive interaction», the Russian patent office (Rospatent) has issued a decision to grant a patent. The solution passed substantive examination: novelty, inventive step, industrial applicability.

A Russian patent has effect in Russia only; the PCT application opens national phases abroad, but there is no such thing as an «international patent».

What the protection covers

The independent claim, stated plainly. Protection attaches to these features, not to the product as a whole.

  • A multimodal coordination core: vision, speech and dialogue converge into one model of the learner instead of being processed separately
  • Speech analysis with prosody — intonation and pace, not just recognised text
  • Adaptation for autism, ADHD and dyslexia with switching between presentation modalities
  • A dialogue spiral: returning to what was not absorbed, with memory management across sessions
  • Protégé mode: the learner explains the material back to the system, inverting the roles
  • Autonomous generation of interface elements driven by the learner’s current state

What the pilot tests

The pilot tests a hypothesis; it does not ship a product. What exactly it tests was written down before the work started.

Hypothesis

Whether the loop «observe the state → adapt the delivery → return to what was missed → have the learner explain it back» works on real people and delivers perceived value.

Sample and material

20–30 students, school algebra: four linked topics with the links made explicit, because the spiral works by returning to earlier material.

How it runs

A single process, browser client, laptop with camera and microphone. Processing happens on the participant’s device; streams are not retained after the session.

What counts as a result

Qualitative feedback from participants. A sample this size cannot yield a statistically significant effect, so we neither promise nor measure double-digit reductions in learning time.

Sign language recognition is not part of the first version: it depends on sign language datasets and runs as a separate track with its own timeline.
