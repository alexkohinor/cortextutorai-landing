What the system does in class

👁️

Notices when a student is lost

From expression, gestures and intonation — before they say so. Most never do.

🔁

Returns to what was missed

A topic left unabsorbed resurfaces in the next session instead of being lost between topics.

🔄

Asks to be taught in return

Protégé mode: the student becomes the explainer. Gaps show up there, not in the exam.

♿

Adjusts the delivery

Profiles for autism, ADHD and dyslexia: what changes is the delivery, not the content.

What it does not do

🚫

It does not grade

Marking stays with you. You will not have to justify a grade an algorithm awarded.

🗂️

It does not rewrite your course

It works inside your material and your order of topics.

🎥

It does not keep video

Processing happens on the participant’s device; streams are not retained after the session.

⏳

None of this works yet

The first version is about a month away. We describe what we are building, not what already ships.
